Still here!

I live quietly.

I overthink, I disappear,
and I build anyway.

Letters, books, music, guitar —
that’s how I release what I don’t say.

I’m a junior web developer and a college student.

Most of my life happens in silence.

I don’t show up often.
I don’t speak unless I have to.
And even when I do,
I leave most things unsaid.

It’s not that there’s nothing inside —
it’s the opposite.

There’s too much.

Too many thoughts,
too many things that don’t settle,
too many things that don’t have a place to go.

I learned to keep them there.

I rarely open up to people.
Once, I did.

I trusted one person enough to give everything —
not just words, but everything I kept hidden.

When it broke,
it didn’t leave the way things usually do.

It stayed.

Not loudly.
Not visibly.
Just something quiet,
always there,
never fully gone.

Since then,
I’ve stopped trying to explain what’s inside.

I carry it instead.

Silently.

Most days, I exist somewhere between thinking and disappearing.
Not fully here,
not fully gone.

Just… present enough.

So I find other ways to let things out.

I write letters I never send.
I read to slow down my mind.
I listen to music when silence becomes too heavy.
Sometimes I play guitar,
just to turn whatever’s inside
into something that doesn’t stay trapped.

I don’t express things the way people expect.

I don’t talk much.
I don’t show much.

But I build.

And whatever I can’t say,
whatever doesn’t fit into words,
whatever stays unfinished inside me —

it ends up there.
